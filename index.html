About

Part developer, part game maker.

I've spent 14+ years building software across hospitality, finance, education and transport. I love working on friendly, well-made products and sharing what I learn along the way.

Outside of work I make small browser games, write, and play more retro video games than I should. That playful side keeps me curious, and it shows up in what I build. You can find my games on itch.io and my writing on Medium.

I'm based in Tokyo, and open to interesting remote roles or a move somewhere new.
